Help shape Plot-it.

A small workspace for artwork, single-line type, and pen plotting. You can help without owning a plotter.

Created by Thierry Charbonnel

Thierry builds and maintains Plot-it. The original application, core, companion, website and documentation are available under Apache 2.0. The OpenPlotFont library and third-party fonts retain their own terms.

Try the alpha

Import an SVG, add text, simulate a drawing, and save your work. Tell us what felt useful, what was confusing, and one feature you would like next.

Share a bug or an idea

A small example and the steps you followed help us understand the problem. Include your browser and operating system. For plotting reports, include the machine and firmware. Review attachments before sharing artwork, fonts, logs or network details.

Contribute code or documentation

Small improvements to documentation, accessibility, import/export and simulation are welcome. Describe the change in an issue before starting a large feature. Hardware profiles remain experimental and require model-specific checks.

Credits and recognition

Plot-it builds on independently licensed work including axi, Plotink, NextDraw homing, OpenPlotFont, HarfBuzz, Clipper, Lucide and the bundled font creators. Their full attributions and licenses are preserved in the third-party notices.

Testing, photos and public name credit are optional. We will ask before featuring a tester’s name, artwork or photographs.
