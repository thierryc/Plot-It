Plot-It / experiment 01

Your pen as a live control

Draw on the pad with your Wacom pen. Watch the input values and a simulated plotter respond to pressure. This experiment sends no commands to a plotter.

Waiting for input. Connect your tablet, then touch the pad with the pen.

Speed changes are smoothed over about 120 ms. Lift the pen, leave the pad, or stop sending input for 500 ms to stop the preview.

Try light and firm strokes, tilt the pen, and press its side buttons. Look for pen as the input type and a pressure value that varies.

Movement is measured in screen pixels, not tablet millimetres. This is hand input, not feedback from the physical plotting pen.
